This page is a visual interaction preview of WhisprTyper’s hold-to-talk flow. It runs entirely in this page, never accesses your microphone, and doesn’t perform live dictation — the real transcription happens in the Mac app.

In the real app, WhisprTyper finalizes your words and returns them to the supported editable field that was active when you started. If that field is unavailable, the transcript stays available in memory.
