On-device by default

Transcribed on your Mac, not on someone’s server

WhisprTyper’s default engine is WhisperKit Multilingual, running locally. The first time you use it, it downloads a speech model of about 626 MB; once cached, transcription happens on your Mac and can work offline.

There is no WhisprTyper account, no ads, no analytics, no tracking, and no developer-operated transcription service. Your audio is used for the current transcription — then it’s gone.

Exact-target insertion

Your words come back to the field they left from

WhisprTyper notes which text field is active the moment recording begins. When you finish, the transcript is inserted right there — it won’t paste into whatever window happens to be in front.

And some fields are simply off-limits: WhisprTyper refuses to type into password fields, full stop.

Recovery & safety

A safety net that forgets on purpose

Wrong window closed, field lost focus, app quit at the wrong moment — it happens. WhisprTyper keeps your most recent transcript in memory only, so you can copy it or retry the insertion from the menu bar.

It’s a safety net, not a log: nothing is written to disk, and the moment you quit the app, it’s gone.

FAQ

Honest answers, before you ask

Which Macs does WhisprTyper support?

WhisprTyper requires macOS 14 (Sonoma) or later. It’s a Mac-only app — there is no Windows, iPhone, or iPad version.

Why is there a 626 MB download on first use?

The default WhisperKit Multilingual engine runs entirely on your Mac, which means the speech model has to live there too. It’s a one-time download of about 626 MB; after that it’s cached locally and reused.

Does it work offline?

Yes — once the model is cached, the default engine transcribes on-device and can work without a network connection. Only the initial model download needs the internet.

What permissions does it need?

Microphone, to hear you, and Accessibility, to insert the finished text into the field you were using. Speech Recognition permission is requested only if you explicitly switch to the optional Apple System Speech engine.

What is the optional Apple System Speech engine?

You can choose macOS’s built-in speech recognition instead of WhisperKit. Be aware that depending on your macOS version and language, Apple’s engine may send audio to Apple’s servers — that processing is governed by Apple’s privacy policy, not ours. The default engine stays local.

What data does WhisprTyper collect about me?

None through the app: no account, no analytics, no tracking, no ads, and no cloud transcript history. Your most recent transcript is held in memory only for recovery and clears when the app quits. The details are in the privacy policy.

Where does my transcript go when I stop talking?

Into the text field that was active when recording began — and only there. WhisprTyper won’t paste into a different destination, and it refuses password fields entirely.
